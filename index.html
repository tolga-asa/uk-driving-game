What changes in the UK

  • Drive on the left. You sit on the right, next to the centre line.
  • Roundabouts go clockwise. Give way to traffic from your right.
  • Right turns cross traffic. Left turns are the short, simple ones.
  • Speeds are in mph. 30 in towns, 60 on single carriageways, 70 on dual carriageways.
  • Red + amber still means stop. No flashing green.
  • Zebra crossings: give way once someone steps on.
